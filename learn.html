Learning paths

Four structured paths from first principles to reading an annual report. Every example uses hypothetical companies or market data at least 30 days old.

These lessons explain concepts. They do not tell you what to buy or sell, and do not consider your personal finances. For personal advice, consult a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser.

Path 1 · Market foundations

LessonWhat you'll learn
1. What is a share?Ownership, shareholders' rights, primary vs secondary market.
2. How exchanges workNSE, BSE, the order book, market and limit orders, T+1 settlement.
3. The market ecosystemRoles of SEBI, exchanges, depositories (NSDL/CDSL), brokers and clearing corporations.
4. Opening a demat accountKYC, nominations, POA/DDPI, and protecting your login.
5. IPOs explainedHow an IPO works, reading an offer document, ASBA, and allotment.
6. IndicesWhat Nifty 50 and Sensex measure and how index weights are calculated.

Path 2 · Reading a business

LessonWhat you'll learn
1. The three statementsProfit & loss, balance sheet and cash flow, and how they link.
2. Key ratiosP/E, P/B, ROE, ROCE, debt-to-equity: what each means and where it misleads.
3. Annual reportsWhere to find them, the MD&A section, related-party notes and auditor remarks.
4. Valuation conceptsIntrinsic value, margin of safety and DCF basics, using a hypothetical Company A.
5. Corporate actionsDividends, bonus issues, splits, buybacks and rights issues.
6. Technical analysis conceptsCharts, trends, support/resistance and volume, all on hypothetical charts.

Path 3 · Mutual funds & ETFs

LessonWhat you'll learn
1. How a mutual fund worksNAV, AMC, trustee, categories as defined by SEBI.
2. Direct vs regular plansExpense ratios and what you pay for.
3. SIP, SWP and STPMechanics, rupee-cost averaging, and its limits.
4. Index funds and ETFsTracking error, liquidity and how they differ.
5. Reading a factsheetRiskometer, portfolio, benchmark and expense ratio.

Path 4 · Risk & investor protection

LessonWhat you'll learn
1. Risk and returnVolatility, drawdowns, and why higher returns always come with higher risk.
2. Diversification and asset allocationConcepts, rebalancing, and matching investments to goals.
3. Derivatives: know the riskHow futures and options work, leverage, and why SEBI studies show most individual F&O traders lose money.
4. Spotting fraudTip groups, 'guaranteed returns', fake apps, pump-and-dump, and how to verify registration.
5. Your rights as an investorGrievance redressal, SCORES, Online Dispute Resolution, and the Investor Charter.
6. Tax basicsSTCG, LTCG, dividends and record-keeping (general concepts, not tax advice).
