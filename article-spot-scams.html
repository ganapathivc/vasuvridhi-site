Seven signs a "stock tip" is a trap

SEBI regularly cautions investors about tips sent through social media groups, messaging apps and unsolicited calls. These are the most common warning signs.

#Red flagWhy it matters
1"Guaranteed" or "fixed" returnsNo one can guarantee market returns.
2Invite-only WhatsApp/Telegram groupsA common route for pump-and-dump schemes.
3Pressure to act "today"Urgency is used to stop you from checking.
4No SEBI registration number, or one that doesn't matchOnly registered IAs/RAs may give advice or recommendations.
5Requests to share login, OTP or trade on your behalfNever share credentials with anyone.
6Apps or websites that aren't from your brokerFake trading apps imitate real brands.
7Screenshots of huge profitsThey are easily fabricated and prove nothing.

How to verify

Search the intermediary's name or registration number on SEBI's list of registered intermediaries. If something has gone wrong with a registered entity, file a complaint on SCORES.
