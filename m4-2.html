A trend is the general direction prices have moved over a period.

TrendDescription (hypothetical)
UptrendA series of higher highs and higher lows
DowntrendA series of lower highs and lower lows
Sideways / rangePrices move back and forth within a band without clear direction

Timeframe changes the picture

The same price history can look like an uptrend on a weekly chart, sideways on a daily chart and like a downtrend on a 15-minute chart. Which "trend" someone talks about depends entirely on the timeframe they chose, and that choice is subjective.

Hypothetical illustration

WeekHighLow
1₹110₹100
2₹116₹104
3₹114₹106
4₹121₹109

Highs and lows are generally rising across weeks, which would be described as an uptrend on a weekly view, even though week 3 was lower than week 2.

Limits

Trends are easy to see in hindsight and hard to identify while they're happening. Trends end, often without warning.

Key takeaways

  • Trends are described by the pattern of highs and lows.
  • The timeframe chosen changes what 'the trend' is.
  • Trends are clear in hindsight, uncertain in real time.
