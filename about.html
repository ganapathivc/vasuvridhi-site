About Vasuvridhi

Vasu (wealth) + vridhi (growth). The growth we aim for is in your understanding. Vasuvridhi exists to help everyday Indians learn how financial markets work, read the numbers for themselves, and recognise risk and fraud.

Who runs it

Vasuvridhi is run by Vasuvridhi Investments Private Limited, based in Thrissur, Kerala. The company is not registered with SEBI in any capacity. Its role is limited to investor education and awareness: explaining concepts, risk and investor rights in simple language.
