Every mutual fund publishes a monthly factsheet. Here's what to look at.

ItemWhat it tells you
Investment objectiveWhat the fund is trying to do
Category & benchmarkWhat it should be compared against
AUMAssets under management, the fund's size
Expense ratio (direct/regular)Annual cost
Portfolio & top holdingsWhere the money is actually invested; concentration
Sector allocationHow diversified it is across industries
Portfolio turnoverHow often holdings change
Standard deviation, beta, Sharpe ratioMeasures of past volatility and risk-adjusted return
For debt funds: YTM, modified duration, credit ratingsInterest-rate sensitivity and credit quality
Past performance vs benchmarkHistorical record. Past performance may not be sustained in future.

The riskometer

SEBI requires every scheme to display a riskometer with six levels:

Level
Low
Low to Moderate
Moderate
Moderately High
High
Very High

The riskometer is reviewed monthly and can change as the portfolio changes. The benchmark also has its own riskometer, shown alongside.

Key takeaways

  • The factsheet shows objective, costs, holdings and risk measures.
  • The riskometer has six levels, from Low to Very High.
  • Past performance is not a reliable guide to future returns.
