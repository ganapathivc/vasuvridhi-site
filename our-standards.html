Our education standards

SEBI distinguishes clearly between investor education and investment advice. Every lesson, article, video and live session we publish follows these rules.

#Rule
1No buy, sell or hold recommendation on any security, directly or indirectly.
2No target prices, entry/exit levels, stop-losses or "levels to watch" for any real security.
3Market price data is used only if it is at least 30 days old, and never to suggest a future price, advice or recommendation. Where possible we use hypothetical data.
4No live or recent prices, tickers or screen-shares of live markets in lessons or sessions.
5No claims of returns, profits, accuracy or "success rates", ours or our learners'.
6No testimonials or screenshots that mention profits or trading results.
7No paid tip groups, signal channels, copy-trading or managing money for anyone.
8No paid promotion of, or referral links to, specific stocks or securities.
9Every page and video clearly states that we are education-only and not SEBI-registered.
10Learners who need personal advice are directed to SEBI-registered Investment Advisers.

If you see any Vasuvridhi content that breaks these rules, please tell us at the grievance page and we will review and correct it.
