SIP basics: how rupee-cost averaging works

Published 18 November 2026 · Education only

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) invests a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals. Because the amount is fixed, you buy more units when the NAV is lower and fewer when it is higher.

Hypothetical illustration

MonthAmountNAV (hypothetical)Units bought
1₹1,000₹5020.00
2₹1,000₹4025.00
3₹1,000₹5020.00
Total₹3,000Avg NAV ₹46.6765.00

The average cost per unit is ₹3,000 ÷ 65 = ₹46.15, slightly below the simple average NAV. This is rupee-cost averaging.

The limits

  • A SIP does not guarantee profit or protect against loss in a falling market.
  • Outcomes depend on the fund, costs and holding period.
  • Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related documents carefully.
