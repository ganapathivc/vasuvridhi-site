Capital losses can reduce your taxable gains, but there are rules on what can be set off against what.

Set-off rules (capital gains)

Type of lossCan be set off against
Short-term capital loss (STCL)Both short-term and long-term capital gains
Long-term capital loss (LTCL)Only long-term capital gains

Capital losses can't be set off against salary or other income.

Carry-forward

  • Unused capital losses can generally be carried forward for 8 assessment years.
  • To carry forward a loss, you must normally file your income-tax return by the due date.

Trading income is different

Intraday equity trading is generally treated as speculative business income, and F&O trading as non-speculative business income. Each has its own set-off rules and may require audit or a different ITR form. These areas involve interpretation; take professional help.

Key takeaways

  • STCL offsets STCG and LTCG; LTCL offsets only LTCG.
  • Losses can be carried forward for 8 years if you file on time.
  • Intraday and F&O are treated as business income.
