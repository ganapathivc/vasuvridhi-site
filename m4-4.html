Volume is the number of shares traded in a period. It is usually shown as bars beneath the price chart.

What volume can tell you

ObservationCommon interpretation
Price moves on high volumeMany participants took part, so the move had wide participation
Price moves on low volumeFewer participants; the move may have had less conviction
Sudden volume spikeOften linked to news, results, index changes or block deals
Consistently low volumeIlliquid share: wider spreads and harder exits (Module 2)

Delivery volume

Exchanges publish the portion of traded quantity that resulted in actual delivery (as opposed to intraday trades squared off the same day). A higher delivery percentage means more shares changed hands for holding rather than just trading.

Caution

Volume can be manipulated. In pump-and-dump schemes, operators trade among themselves to create the appearance of activity and attract others (Module 8). Unusual volume in a small, little-known company, combined with tips circulating on social media, is a warning sign, not an opportunity.

Key takeaways

  • Volume = shares traded in a period.
  • It shows how many participated in a move.
  • Volume can be faked in manipulated shares.
