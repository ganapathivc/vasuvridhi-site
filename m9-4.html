Good records make tax filing accurate and protect you if questions arise later.

DocumentWhere to get itWhy
Contract notesBroker (email)Proof of every trade, price and charges
Capital gains / tax P&L statementBroker back-officeSummarises STCG/LTCG for the year
Mutual fund capital gains statementCAMS / KFintech / AMCGains on fund redemptions
Consolidated Account Statement (CAS)NSDL / CDSLAll holdings across demat accounts and funds
Dividend statementsCompanies / RTA / bankDividend income and TDS
AIS / TIS and Form 26ASIncome-tax e-filing portalWhat the tax department already knows about you
Bank statementsBankFund flows to and from your broker
Purchase records for old holdingsOld contract notes, CASCost of acquisition for long-held shares

Good habits

  • Download statements at the end of each financial year (31 March), before platforms archive them.
  • Reconcile your broker's statement with AIS before filing, and resolve mismatches.
  • Keep records for at least as long as the tax department can reopen assessments. Many people keep them for 8+ years.

Key takeaways

  • Keep contract notes, capital gains statements and CAS.
  • Reconcile with AIS/26AS before filing.
  • Archive records yearly.
