The profit & loss statement (P&L, or income statement) shows how much a company earned and spent over a period: a quarter or a year.

EPS (on 10 crore shares)₹11.20PAT ÷ number of shares

Useful margins

  • EBITDA margin = 220 ÷ 1,000 = 22%
  • Net profit margin = 112 ÷ 1,000 = 11.2%

What to watch

  • Trend: one year means little. Compare 5 years or more.
  • Other income: if profit depends heavily on it, the core business may be weaker than it looks.
  • Exceptional items: one-time gains or losses distort comparisons.
  • Standalone vs consolidated: consolidated figures include subsidiaries and usually give the fuller picture.

Key takeaways

  • P&L = revenue minus expenses over a period.
  • Look at margins and multi-year trends.
  • Separate one-time items from recurring profit.
